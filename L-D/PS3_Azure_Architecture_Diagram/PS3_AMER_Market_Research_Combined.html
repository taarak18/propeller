🔲 10. SWOT — AMER Combined L&D Market Entry

💪 Strengths

  • Only AMER platform combining compliance + upskilling + risk engine + interventions
  • CHIPS Act module — unique; required for $52B in federal funding recipients
  • SEC Cybersecurity competency tracking — legally required from 2024
  • GenAI literacy tracking — 71% of workers using AI untrained; urgent demand
  • Self-service SaaS at $14/user vs 6–18 months for Workday/SAP at $25–$65

⚠️ Weaknesses

  • Zero AMER brand recognition or customer references at launch
  • No native content — requires LMS integration (Coursera, LinkedIn Learning)
  • US enterprise sales cycles 6–12 months
  • FedRAMP certification not yet started — US gov sector blocked initially
  • Canada bilingual (EN/FR) requirement adds complexity

🌱 Opportunities

  • CHIPS Act — 50,000+ new US jobs need tracked training plans NOW
  • SEC Cybersecurity rules — every public company needs board cyber training docs
  • 87M US workers need reskilling by 2030 — persistent multi-year demand
  • Workday Skills Cloud gaps — position as Workday compliance + skills add-on
  • LatAm: Brazil LGPD + upskilling — emerging $3B market for Year 3+

🚨 Threats

  • Workday/SAP expanding compliance + skills features — fast follower risk
  • Degreed and EdCast adding compliance modules
  • Microsoft Viva Learning expanding skills analytics
  • CCPA+ state privacy laws adding compliance complexity
  • Economic slowdown reducing discretionary upskilling budgets
