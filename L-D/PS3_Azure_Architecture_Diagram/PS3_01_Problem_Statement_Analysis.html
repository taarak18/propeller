☁️ SaaS Platform Overview

This system is designed and delivered as a multi-tenant SaaS (Software as a Service) platform. Multiple organizations (Tenants) subscribe and operate within a fully isolated, independently configurable environment — sharing underlying cloud infrastructure. The platform provider manages hosting, upgrades, scaling, and availability.

🚨 1. What Is the Problem Statement

Organizations are accountable for employee learning outcomes, training attendance, and compliance with regulatory bodies — yet learning data is fragmented across LMS platforms, assessment systems, and trainer notes. Employees falling behind are detected too late, and interventions are reactive rather than proactive.
SaaS Perspective: This problem exists across hundreds of organizations simultaneously. A SaaS platform allows organizations to solve this through a shared, always-on, continuously improving service — eliminating the cost and complexity of building internal solutions. Every improvement benefits all tenants.

Root Causes

  • Fragmented Data: Learning data spread across multiple disconnected systems per organization
  • Reactive Detection: At-risk employees identified only after failure — no early warning system
  • No Unified Profile: No consolidated view of an employee's learning journey
  • Manual Compliance Reporting: Error-prone, slow, and costly across organizations
  • Untracked Interventions: Remedial efforts are informal; effectiveness not measured
  • High Cost per Org: Each organization builds bespoke tools — expensive and non-scalable

A multi-tenant SaaS platform for corporate learning progress tracking, at-risk learner identification, intervention management, and compliance reporting — serving multiple organizations through a subscription model.

📌 7. Assumptions & Constraints

✅ Assumptions

  • Tenants self-register and onboard via the platform portal
  • Each tenant's source systems expose REST APIs
  • Tenant data isolated via Row-Level Security (RLS)
  • Billing managed externally (Stripe / Chargebee API)
  • SSO uses SAML 2.0 or OIDC standards
  • Platform SLA: 99.9% uptime guaranteed
  • All data encrypted at rest and in transit
  • Each tenant independently manages their risk rules

⚠️ Constraints

  • Not a full LMS — tracking and analytics layer only
  • Phase 1: web-only (no native mobile app)
  • Rule engine is rule-based — no ML/AI in Phase 1
  • White-labelling limited to Enterprise tier (Phase 2)
  • In-app billing portal deferred to Phase 2
  • Multi-region data residency deferred to Phase 3
  • 20-week Phase 1 delivery timeline
  • Single-region cloud deployment in Phase 1
